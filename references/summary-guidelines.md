# Summary Guidelines

Use these rules when writing the note sections before the archived original article.

## Goal

Write notes that help future reading and retrieval. Avoid generic summaries that only say "the article discusses..." without preserving the author's actual claims.

Batch archiving follows the same standard as single-article archiving. Never lower the bar because there are many links.

## Required Sections

### 摘要

Write 150-250 Chinese characters. Answer four questions in prose:

1. What is the article's core claim?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What evidence, case, or reasoning does the author use?
4. What can the reader take away?

Do not repeat the title as the first sentence. Do not add claims not supported by the article.

Do not use the article's opening paragraphs as the summary. Before writing, inspect the article's beginning, section headings, middle examples, and conclusion. The summary must synthesize the whole article, not merely restate the lead.

### 核心要点

Write 3-5 bullets. Each bullet should contain both a claim and a concrete reason, example, or implication.

Good:

```markdown
- AI 工具的价值不在替代专业判断，而在放大业务专家的判断速度；文章用企业场景说明，没有业务上下文的自动化很容易变成低质量产出。
```

Weak:

```markdown
- 文章介绍了 AI 工具的价值。
```

### 这篇解决什么问题

Use one sentence. Phrase it as a reader problem:

```markdown
帮助读者判断在 AI 工具快速变化时，个人应该把学习重点放在工具操作、业务理解还是判断框架上。
```

### 快速判断

Keep it practical:

- 适合人群: roles or reader types
- 适合场景: decisions, projects, or learning moments
- 不适合场景: when the article is too introductory, too opinionated, too tactical, or not immediately useful

## Style

- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Preserve the author's central distinction or framework if there is one.
- Mention important numbers, product names, companies, or models when they drive the argument.
- If the article is mostly news, summarize event, actor, change, impact, and uncertainty.
- If the article is a tutorial, summarize objective, method, prerequisites, and output.
- If the article is opinion or analysis, summarize thesis, reasoning chain, and practical implication.
- If the article is about business, summarize the business question, the author's judgment, the company/industry evidence, the causal logic, and the transferable lesson. Do not stop at describing the case.

## Do Not

- Do not invent background not in the article.
- Do not over-flatten disagreement or uncertainty.
- Do not write marketing copy.
- Do not replace the original body with your summary.
- Do not include emoji in section headings; match the vault convention.
- Do not use boilerplate patterns across a batch, especially `文章围绕“{title}”展开，核心内容是...`.
- Do not write `核心要点` bullets from only the first few paragraphs; each bullet should reflect a distinct part of the article.
